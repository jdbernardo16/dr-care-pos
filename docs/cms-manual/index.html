Getting Started CMS Manual 04 / 41
I.A. Logging In
Navigate to your system URL to access the Dr Care admin panel. The login page is your entry point to all management features.
Step 1
Navigate to the /sign-in page in your browser.
Step 2
Enter your username (e.g., "admin") in the username field.
Step 3
Enter your secure password in the password field.
Step 4
Click the "Sign In" button to proceed to the dashboard.
Note
Default credentials are provided by your system administrator. Contact your admin if you need a password reset.
Getting Started CMS Manual 05 / 41
I.B. Dashboard Overview
After login you land on the dashboard, which provides a real-time snapshot of your business performance through various widgets and charts.
Step 1
View key widgets: Incomplete Orders, Income, Best Customers, Expenses, and Best Cashiers.
Step 2
Monitor the Weekly Sales chart for trend analysis.
Step 3
Check Recent Orders to track latest transactions.
Step 4
Click Home in the sidebar at any time to return to the dashboard.
Note
Widgets auto-refresh periodically. Use the refresh buttons on each card to manually update data.
Getting Started CMS Manual 06 / 41
I.C. Point of Sale (POS)
The Point of Sale interface is the main order-entry interface where cashiers process customer transactions.
Step 1
Click POS in the sidebar to open the order-entry screen.
Step 2
Use the search bar to find products and add them to the cart.
Step 3
Select a customer for the transaction.
Step 4
Choose a payment method and complete the sale.
Note
The POS is the main order-entry interface. All sales must go through this screen.

Inventory & Procurement CMS Manual 14 / 41
III.A. Product Catalog
The Product Catalog is the central repository for all items you sell. Each product must be configured with categories, units, and pricing before it can be sold.
Step 1
Navigate to Inventory → Products to view the product catalog.
Step 2
Click "Create Product" to add a new item.
Step 3
Fill in the product name, SKU, barcode, and tax settings.
Step 4
Assign the product to a category and unit group.
Note
Products must belong to a category and have units assigned before they can be sold in the POS.
Inventory & Procurement CMS Manual 15 / 41
III.B. Categories
Categories organize your products into logical groups, making it easier to browse, filter, and manage your inventory.
Step 1
Navigate to Inventory → Categories.
Step 2
Organize products into categories (e.g., Medications, Supplies, Equipment).
Step 3
Create parent-child category hierarchies for sub-categorization.
Step 4
Assign products to categories during product creation or editing.
Inventory & Procurement CMS Manual 16 / 41
III.C. Units & Conversions
Units define how products are measured and sold. Unit groups allow the same product to be sold in multiple units with conversion rates.
Step 1
Define measurement units: pieces, boxes, kilograms, liters, etc.
Step 2
Group related units into unit groups (e.g., "Weight" with kg and g).
Step 3
Set conversion rates between units within a group (e.g., 1 box = 12 pieces).
Step 4
Assign unit groups to products for multi-unit selling capability.
Note
Unit groups allow selling the same product in different units. For example, a product can be sold both as a single piece and by the box.
Inventory & Procurement CMS Manual 17 / 41
III.D. Stock Management
Stock management tools let you adjust inventory levels, set scale ranges for weighted products, and maintain a full audit trail of stock movements.
Step 1
Adjust stock manually for inventory corrections or initial stock setup.
Step 2
Set scale range for weight-based products sold by weight.
Step 3
View stock flow records for a complete audit trail.
Stock adjustments are recorded in product history for full traceability and auditing.
Inventory & Procurement CMS Manual 18 / 41
III.E. Labels Printing
The Labels Printing feature lets you generate barcode and product labels for your inventory items directly from the system.
Step 1
Select the products you need labels for.
Step 2
Choose the unit for which to print labels.
Step 3
Configure the label layout and size.
Step 4
Print barcode or label sheets for physical product tagging.
Inventory & Procurement CMS Manual 19 / 41
III.F. Procurement (Purchasing)
The Procurement module manages the purchasing workflow — ordering stock from suppliers and recording the cost of goods sold.
Step 1
Navigate to Procurements → Procurements List.
Step 2
Click "New Procurement" to start a purchase order.
Step 3
Select a provider, add products with quantities and purchase prices.
Step 4
Submit the procurement to increase stock levels.
Note
Procurement records the cost of goods (purchase_price) which flows into COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) calculations for profit reporting.
Inventory & Procurement CMS Manual 20 / 41
III.G. Provider Management
The Providers module stores your supplier and vendor information. Maintain contact details and address for all companies you purchase from.
Step 1
Navigate to Providers → List to view all suppliers.
Step 2
Create new provider records with company name and contact details.
Step 3
Store provider address, phone, and email information.
Step 4
Select providers during procurement to track purchasing history.

Financial Management CMS Manual 22 / 41
IV.A. Transactions (Expenses)
Record all business expenses to track outgoing funds. Each expense is categorized by account for accurate financial reporting.
Step 1
Navigate to Accounting → Expenses.
Step 2
Record business expenses with amount, date, and description.
Step 3
Categorize each expense by selecting the appropriate account.
Note
Every transaction affects your account balances. Ensure expenses are categorized correctly for accurate financial reports.
Financial Management CMS Manual 23 / 41
IV.B. Accounts
Define your chart of accounts to categorize all financial transactions. Accounts organize your finances into assets, liabilities, income, and expense categories.
Step 1
Navigate to Accounting → Accounts.
Step 2
Define accounts for different financial categories (assets, liabilities, income, expenses).
Step 3
Create parent-child account hierarchies as needed.
Step 4
Assign transactions to accounts for accurate categorization.
Financial Management CMS Manual 24 / 41
IV.C. Tax Management
Tax management lets you define tax groups, add individual taxes, and assign them to products. Proper tax configuration ensures accurate pricing and reporting.
Step 1
Create tax groups (e.g., VAT) that bundle multiple tax rates together.
Step 2
Add individual taxes to each group with specific rates.
Step 3
Assign tax groups to products during product configuration.
Step 4
Set taxes as inclusive or exclusive of the product price.
Note
Tax configuration affects pricing and reporting accuracy. Inclusive taxes are included in the displayed price; exclusive taxes are added at checkout.
