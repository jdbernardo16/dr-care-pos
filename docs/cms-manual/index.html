Sales & Operations
Manage daily sales, process orders, and configure payment types from a unified operations hub
💵
Sales & Operations CMS Manual 04 / 35
I.A. Logging In
Access the Dr Care POS admin panel through the sign-in page. This is the secure entry point for all administrative functions.
Step 1
Navigate to the admin sign-in URL at /sign-in on your Dr Care domain.
Step 2
Enter your username in the Username field.
Step 3
Enter your password in the Password field.
Step 4
Click the "Sign In" button to authenticate and access the dashboard.
Note
Keep your credentials secure. The system does not expose password reset from the login screen by default. Contact a system administrator if you are locked out.
Sales & Operations CMS Manual 05 / 35
I.B. Dashboard Overview
The Dashboard is your command center. It provides at-a-glance metrics, recent orders, best customers, and quick access to all modules.
Step 1
Review the summary cards: Incomplete Orders, Expenses, Income, and Weekly Sales.
Step 2
Check the "Recents Orders" panel for the latest transactions with timestamps and totals.
Step 3
Use the left sidebar to navigate to any module. Expandable menus reveal sub-pages.
Step 4
Click the notification bell to view system alerts and low-stock warnings.
The dashboard auto-refreshes metrics when you return from other modules. Use it as your daily starting point.
Sales & Operations CMS Manual 06 / 35
I.C. Point of Sale
The POS screen is where front-desk staff process sales. It combines product browsing, cart management, and checkout in one interface.
Step 1
Navigate to the POS module from the sidebar or use /dashboard/pos.
Step 2
Browse products by category or search by name. Click a product to add it to the cart.
Step 3
Adjust quantities, apply discounts, or remove items directly from the cart panel.
Step 4
Select a customer, choose the payment type, and complete the order.
Note
The POS supports multiple payment methods and can print receipts automatically if a thermal printer is configured in Settings.
Sales & Operations CMS Manual 07 / 35
I.D. Managing Orders
The Orders List gives you a complete history of every transaction. Filter, search, and review order details from one centralized table.
Step 1
Open the Orders List from the sidebar to see all completed, hold, and refunded orders.
Step 2
Use the search bar and date filters to narrow down specific transactions.
Step 3
Click any order row to open the detail view, showing items, customer, and payment breakdown.
Step 4
Use action buttons to refund, print receipts, or re-process an order.
Orders are immutable once completed. Refunds generate a separate record for audit purposes.

Inventory & Products
Manage your product catalog, stock levels, categories, units, providers, and procurement workflows
📦
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 09 / 35
II.A. Product Catalog
The Products List is the backbone of your inventory. Every item available for sale is created, edited, and monitored from this screen.
Step 1
Navigate to Inventory > Products to view the full catalog with stock counts and pricing.
Step 2
Use filters to show products by category, stock status, or visibility.
Step 3
Click the "Create Product" button to add a new item to the catalog.
Step 4
Hover over a product row to reveal quick-action icons: edit, delete, or adjust stock.
Note
Products with low stock appear highlighted. Set reorder thresholds in the product detail form to receive automatic alerts.
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 10 / 35
II.B. Creating a Product
The product creation form captures all details needed to sell an item: identity, pricing, stock, category, and unit of measure.
Step 1
Enter the product name, SKU, and barcode. These identifiers must be unique across the catalog.
Step 2
Assign a category and unit group. Select the default unit (e.g., piece, box, kg).
Step 3
Set the sale price, wholesale price, and purchase price for margin tracking.
Step 4
Upload a product image, set initial stock quantity, and enable or disable the product.
After saving, the product appears immediately in the POS and can be sold. You can edit it anytime from the product list.
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 11 / 35
II.C. Product Categories
Categories group products for easier browsing in the POS and cleaner reporting. They also drive menu organization on the sales floor.
Step 1
Navigate to Inventory > Categories to see the current category tree.
Step 2
Click "Create Category" to add a new group. Provide a name, description, and optional parent category.
Step 3
Upload a category image if you want visual tiles in the POS category picker.
Step 4
Use drag handles to reorder categories. Child categories inherit the parent's display settings.
Note
Deleting a category does not delete its products. Items will simply show as uncategorized until reassigned.
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 12 / 35
II.D. Stock Adjustment
Stock Adjustment allows you to correct inventory counts without a sale or procurement. Use it for damage, spoilage, or cycle-count corrections.
Step 1
Go to Inventory > Stock Adjustment. Select the product that needs a quantity correction.
Step 2
Enter the new actual quantity. The system calculates the difference and records the adjustment reason.
Step 3
Choose an adjustment type: Damaged, Lost, Found, or General Correction.
Step 4
Save the adjustment. The product's stock updates immediately and the change is logged in Stock Flow Records.
Note
Always add a descriptive note. Auditors and managers rely on these notes to understand why inventory changed outside normal sales.
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 13 / 35
II.E. Stock Flow Records
Stock Flow Records provide an immutable audit trail of every unit that entered or left your inventory. This is critical for reconciliation and compliance.
Step 1
Navigate to Inventory > Stock Flow Records to view the chronological history.
Step 2
Filter by product, operation type (sale, procurement, adjustment), or date range.
Step 3
Each row shows the product, quantity delta, operation, user who performed it, and timestamp.
Step 4
Export the filtered view to CSV for external accounting or warehouse reconciliation.
Records are read-only. If a mistake is found, perform a reverse adjustment rather than editing history.
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 14 / 35
II.F. Managing Providers
Providers are your suppliers and vendors. Maintaining accurate provider records ensures smooth procurement and accurate cost tracking.
Step 1
Open the Providers List from the sidebar to see all registered suppliers.
Step 2
Click "Create A Provider" to add a new supplier. Record name, contact person, phone, email, and address.
Step 3
Assign a provider to products in the product form to link procurement history.
Step 4
Use the provider detail page to view all procurements and outstanding balances.
Note
Inactive providers can be disabled rather than deleted. This preserves historical procurement records for reporting.
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 15 / 35
II.G. Procurements
The Procurements module tracks every purchase from your providers. It updates inventory and records the cost basis for margin calculations.
Step 1
Navigate to Procurements > Procurements List to review past and pending purchases.
Step 2
Click "New Procurement" to create a purchase order. Select the provider first.
Step 3
Add products by scanning barcode or selecting from the catalog. Enter quantities and unit costs.
Step 4
Save as Draft for approval, or mark as Delivered to immediately add stock to inventory.
Delivered procurements are irreversible. If you make an error, create a stock adjustment to correct the count.
Inventory & Products CMS Manual 16 / 35
II.H. New Procurement
The procurement creation form is designed for speed. Add multiple products, apply provider discounts, and record shipping costs in one workflow.
Step 1
Choose the provider from the dropdown. The system loads their product history for quick re-ordering.
Step 2
Click "Add Product" and search by name or SKU. Specify quantity and per-unit cost.
Step 3
Review the summary panel: subtotal, tax, discount, shipping, and grand total.
Step 4
Set the procurement status and delivery date, then save. Stock updates on delivery confirmation.
Note
If a product is new to the system, create it in the catalog first before adding it to a procurement.

Customers & CRM CMS Manual 18 / 35
III.A. Customer Directory
The Customers module stores every person or organization that has purchased from your business. Use it for service history, outreach, and loyalty tracking.
Step 1
Open Customers > List to see the full directory with contact info and total purchase history.
Step 2
Search by name, phone, or email to find a specific customer quickly.
Step 3
Click a customer row to open their profile: orders, rewards balance, and group membership.
Step 4
Use action buttons to edit details, assign a group, or view their complete order timeline.
Note
Customers created during POS checkout are automatically added to the directory with basic contact info.
Customers & CRM CMS Manual 19 / 35
III.B. Adding a Customer
Manually add customers for pre-registration, corporate accounts, or patients who prefer walk-in registration before their first purchase.
Step 1
Click "Create Customer" from the customer list or sidebar.
Step 2
Fill in the name, email, and phone. These fields are required for order association and receipts.
Step 3
Select a customer group if applicable (e.g., VIP, Corporate, Senior). This affects pricing rules.
Step 4
Add an address and any internal notes, then save. The customer is now selectable at checkout.
You can import customers in bulk via CSV from the customer list action menu.
Customers & CRM CMS Manual 20 / 35
III.C. Customer Groups
Groups let you apply tiered pricing, discounts, and access rules to segments of your customer base. Common groups include VIP, Staff, Corporate, and Senior.
Step 1
Navigate to Customers > Customers Groups to see existing segments.
Step 2
Click "Create Group" to define a new segment. Name it descriptively and set a default discount percentage.
Step 3
Assign customers to the group from their individual profile pages or via bulk selection in the list.
Step 4
When a grouped customer is selected at POS, their discount auto-applies to eligible products.
Note
Group discounts stack with coupon codes. Be careful when combining multiple discount layers to avoid negative margins.

Accounting & Finance CMS Manual 22 / 35
IV.A. Expenses & Transactions
Record every business expense in the Transactions module. This feeds directly into profit-and-loss reporting and ensures accurate bookkeeping.
Step 1
Navigate to Accounting > Expenses to view the transaction log.
Step 2
Click "Create Expense" to record a new outgoing payment. Choose the category and linked account.
Step 3
Enter the amount, description, and date. Attach a receipt image for digital record-keeping.
Step 4
Use the Transaction History sub-page to review past entries by date range or category.
Note
Expenses are not the same as procurements. Procurements add inventory; expenses are operational outflows like rent or utilities.
Accounting & Finance CMS Manual 23 / 35
IV.B. Accounts Management
Accounts represent your financial ledgers: cash drawers, bank accounts, mobile wallets, and credit lines. They are the source and destination of all money flows.
Step 1
Go to Accounting > Accounts to see all registered financial accounts with current balances.
Step 2
Click "Create Account" to add a new ledger. Choose the type: Cash, Bank, or Credit.
Step 3
Name the account descriptively (e.g., "Main Cash Drawer", "BPI Checking"). Set an opening balance.
Step 4
Link accounts to payment types so that POS transactions auto-deposit to the correct ledger.
Account balances update in real time as sales and expenses are recorded across the system.
Accounting & Finance CMS Manual 24 / 35
IV.C. Tax Configuration
Taxes are calculated automatically at checkout based on the tax groups and rates you define here. Ensure compliance with local tax regulations.
Step 1
Navigate to Taxes > Taxes to see existing rates. Rates are grouped for easier application.
Step 2
Click "Create Tax" to add a new rate. Provide a name, percentage, and optional tax number.
Step 3
Create Tax Groups to bundle multiple taxes (e.g., VAT + Local Tax) that apply together.
Step 4
Assign tax groups to products or categories. The POS auto-calculates the correct total at checkout.
Note
Tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive modes are set in Settings > POS. Verify this before your first sale to avoid pricing errors.
Accounting & Finance CMS Manual 25 / 35
IV.D. Creating a Tax
The tax creation form lets you define the rate, type, and applicability rules for any government or local levy.
Step 1
Enter a descriptive name such as "VAT 12%" or "Service Charge 5%".
Step 2
Choose the tax type: Percentage, Fixed Amount, or Compound (applies on top of another tax).
Step 3
Set the rate value. For percentages, enter 12 for 12%. For fixed, enter the exact amount in Php.
Step 4
Decide if the tax is included in the displayed price or added at checkout. Save to activate.
You can override tax settings per product if certain items are exempt or subject to a different rate.
Accounting & Finance CMS Manual 26 / 35
IV.E. Sales Reports
Sales Reports turn raw transaction data into actionable insights. Filter by date, product, cashier, or payment type to understand performance.
Step 1
Navigate to Reports > Sale Report to open the primary sales analytics dashboard.
Step 2
Set the date range using the picker. Choose preset ranges like Today, This Week, or Custom.
Step 3
Review the summary cards: total sales, tax collected, refunds, and net revenue.
Step 4
Scroll to the breakdown tables: top products, top categories, and sales by hour.
Note
All report tables can be exported to CSV or PDF. Use the export button in the top-right of each table.

Staff & System CMS Manual 28 / 35
V.A. User Management
The Users module controls who can access the admin panel. Each user gets a role that defines their permissions across all modules.
Step 1
Navigate to Users > Users List to see all active and inactive system users.
Step 2
Click "Create User" to add a new staff member. Enter username, email, and assign a role.
Step 3
Set a secure temporary password. The user will be prompted to change it on first login.
Step 4
Enable or disable accounts without deleting them. Disabled users cannot log in but retain audit history.
Note
Never share admin accounts. Every action is logged by user ID, and shared credentials break audit trails.
Staff & System CMS Manual 29 / 35
V.B. Creating Users
The user creation form captures identity, role, and initial credentials. A well-defined role ensures the user only accesses what they need.
Step 1
Fill in the required fields: username, email, and password. The username must be unique.
Step 2
Select a role from the dropdown. Common roles include Admin, Cashier, Inventory Manager, and Accountant.
Step 3
Add optional details: full name, phone, and avatar image for staff identification.
Step 4
Save the user. An account activation email is sent automatically if SMTP is configured in Settings.
After creation, the user appears in the list and can log in immediately using the provided credentials.
Staff & System CMS Manual 30 / 35
V.C. Role Permissions
Roles define what a user can see and do. The system ships with default roles, but you can create custom ones for fine-grained access control.
Step 1
Navigate to Users > Roles to view existing roles. Click a role to inspect its current permission set.
Step 2
Click "Create Roles" to define a new role. Name it clearly (e.g., "Senior Cashier").
Step 3
Use the Permissions Manager to toggle access per module: Read, Create, Update, Delete, and Export.
Step 4
Assign the new role to users from their profile or during user creation.
Note
The "Admin" role has full system access and cannot be restricted. Create secondary admin roles with narrower scope for daily operations.
Staff & System CMS Manual 31 / 35
V.D. Attendance Records
Track staff punctuality and working hours with the Attendance module. Records feed directly into Payroll calculations.
Step 1
Navigate to Attendance > Records to view the daily check-in and check-out log.
Step 2
Staff clock in via the "Clock In/Out" page. Managers can also create manual entries for corrections.
Step 3
Filter records by user, date range, or status (Present, Late, Absent, On Leave).
Step 4
Export attendance summaries to CSV for integration with external payroll systems if needed.
Note
Overtime entries are managed separately in the Overtime module and are linked to attendance records for validation.
Staff & System CMS Manual 32 / 35
V.E. Payroll Runs
Payroll Runs calculate gross pay, deductions, and net pay for each staff member based on attendance and approved overtime.
Step 1
Navigate to Payroll > Pay Runs to see existing payroll periods.
Step 2
Click "Create Pay Run" to start a new period. Select the date range and eligible employees.
Step 3
Review the auto-calculated summary: regular hours, overtime, deductions, and net pay per employee.
Step 4
Approve the pay run to finalize. Approved runs are locked and generate printable payslips.
Once approved, a pay run cannot be edited. Create an adjustment run if corrections are needed later.
Staff & System CMS Manual 33 / 35
V.F. Media Library
The Media Library is a centralized repository for all images and files used across products, categories, and receipts.
Step 1
Navigate to Medias from the sidebar to open the media manager.
Step 2
Upload files by dragging and dropping into the upload zone or clicking the Upload button.
Step 3
Organize files into folders. Supported formats include JPG, PNG, GIF, and PDF.
Step 4
Click any file to copy its URL or insert it directly into a product or category form.
Note
Large images are automatically resized into thumbnails. Keep originals under 2 MB for optimal upload speed.
Staff & System CMS Manual 34 / 35
V.G. Modules
NexoPOS supports modular extensions. The Modules page lets you install, enable, disable, and update add-ons that extend Dr Care's functionality.
Step 1
Navigate to Modules > List to see installed modules and their status (active or inactive).
Step 2
Click "Upload Module" to install a new extension. Upload the module ZIP file provided by the developer.
Step 3
Toggle the module switch to enable or disable it. Disabling preserves settings but hides features.
Step 4
Check for updates on the module card. Updated modules may require a system refresh after installation.
Note
Only install modules from trusted sources. Third-party modules have full database access and can affect system stability.
Staff & System CMS Manual 35 / 35
V.H. General Settings
General Settings control the core behavior of Dr Care: store identity, currency, language, date format, and receipt templates.
Step 1
Navigate to Settings > General to open the primary configuration panel.
Step 2
Set the store name, address, and contact details. These appear on printed receipts and reports.
Step 3
Configure currency settings: symbol (Php), decimal separator, thousand separator, and precision.
Step 4
Review sub-settings pages: POS, Customers, Orders, Accounting, Reports, Invoices, and Reset.
Note
Changes to currency or date format take effect immediately across all screens. Warn active cashiers before modifying these values.
