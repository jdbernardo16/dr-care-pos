Key Differences I Noticed

Loyverse strengths to consider:

  • Cleaner cart layout — simpler item rows (no separate Qty/Total columns)
  • Single "Charge" button — prominent, full-width, shows total amount
  • Minimal header — no toolbar buttons cluttering the cart area
  • Cart on the left — feels like a sidebar panel, products on right
  • Category chips — pill-shaped filters at top of product area

Things to preserve:

  • Comments, Taxes, Coupons — need to live somewhere accessible
  • Hold functionality — important for clinic workflow
  • Customer selection — tied to orders
  • Order type toggle (walk-in, delivery, etc.)
  • Discount and void capabilities
